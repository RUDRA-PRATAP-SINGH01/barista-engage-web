# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name

Barista Engage

AI-Native CRM & Marketing Intelligence Platform for Coffee Chains

---

## Product Vision

Barista Engage enables coffee brands to understand customers, create intelligent audience segments, generate personalized campaigns, simulate delivery outcomes, and receive AI-powered marketing insights.

The platform combines traditional CRM capabilities with AI-powered decision support.

The goal is not to replace marketers.

The goal is to help marketers:

* identify the right audience
* create better campaigns
* understand campaign performance
* improve future campaign outcomes

---

## Primary User

Marketing Manager

Responsibilities:

* Discover customer audiences
* Create campaigns
* Analyze campaign performance
* Improve retention
* Reduce churn

---

## Core Product Pillars

### 1. Customer Intelligence

Provides visibility into:

* Customer profiles
* Loyalty information
* RFM scores
* Churn risk
* Favorite products
* Customer personas
* Marketing summaries

---

### 2. Audience Discovery

Marketers can:

* Create manual segments
* Preview audience size
* Explore sample customers
* Use AI Audience Builder

Example:

"Find cold brew lovers who haven't visited in two months"

---

### 3. Campaign Management

Marketers can:

* Create campaigns
* Select audience segments
* Select communication channel
* Launch campaigns
* Track delivery status

Supported channels:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* SMS

---

### 4. Campaign Analytics

Track:

* Audience size
* Delivery rate
* Open rate
* Click rate
* Segment breakdown
* Communication funnel
